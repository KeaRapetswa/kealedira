About Us

Company Overview

Kealedira Holdings is a specialized meat distribution enterprise focused on supplying high-volume consumer favorites polony, russians, and viennas. Recognizing the constant demand for reliable, affordable, and safe processed meat products in retail and food service markets, we built a logistics network engineered specifically for chilled goods handling. Through strict hygiene protocols, state-of-the-art cold storage, and strategic delivery routes, we ensure seamlessly managed supply chains from production facilities straight to point-of-sale.

Our Mission & Vision


Our Mission

To be the most trusted and efficient distributor of polony, russians, and viennas by providing uncompromising cold-chain integrity, exceptional service, and consistent stock availability to all retail partners.

Our Vision


To build the leading regional distribution footprint for processed meats, empowering local retailers, butcheries, and food businesses with quality products and dependable supply networks.

Core Values


Cold-Chain Safety: Strict adherence to temperature-controlled storage and transport standards.

Reliability: Guaranteed scheduling so your customers always find what they need.

Quality Assurance: Partnering exclusively with certified producers of high-standard processed meats.

Service Excellence: Building strong, long-term relationships with both large-scale retailers and local merchants.
